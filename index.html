(About_Kortalan Forma)Using raw materials like volcanic stone and wood, Kortalan Forma Studio's sculptural forms blur the line between Furniture and Collectible Design. Collaborating closely with talented craftsmen in Mexico each piece is worked by hand to reveal the beauty in the incredible materials found there.

Founded by designer Marc Personick, Kortalan Forma launched its initial collection in 2023, often partnering with other talented architects and designers around the world with the direction of his brutalist aesthetic.

The resulting furniture and objects are simultaneously functional pieces and collectible art.
